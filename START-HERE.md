# Start Here

Prepared by Nisar Muhammad.

1. [Download the eight-page PDF](https://github.com/nisarnis-prog/Create-Cinematic-Reels-With-Codex/releases/download/v1.0.0/Create-Cinematic-Reels-With-Codex.pdf) and follow it in order.
2. Open [COPY-PASTE-PROMPTS.md](COPY-PASTE-PROMPTS.md). Use **Prompt 1 in Codex** with AI_Video_Editor selected.
3. Confirm that the short test MP4 renders and plays. Review permission requests.
4. Use **Prompt 2 in ChatGPT** to approve narration and visuals and prepare a Codex brief.
5. Paste the brief and **Prompt 3 into Codex** and review the animated sample.
6. Optionally use **Prompt 4 in Codex** for ElevenLabs, after approving the script and credits, or provide your own voice recording.
7. Once the narration and sample are approved, use **Prompt 5 in Codex** for final production.
8. Request timestamp-based corrections using **Prompt 6 in Codex**.

Find exported review and final MP4s in the project’s Output folder. Ask Codex for the exact filename and review the export on your phone before posting. Read the [safety precautions and responsibilities](README.md#safety-precautions) first.
